# Synthetic refund policy

Synthetic demonstration data only.

The customer-support AI use case may:

- summarize ticket and account context;
- draft a customer reply;
- recommend that a refund be reviewed.

The customer-support AI use case may not execute `issue_refund`.

Every refund requires a human support lead. Refunds above EUR 100 additionally require finance approval. The customer must not be told that a refund is complete until the payment system records the approved action.
