# ACME synthetic vendor review policy

A first-pass review must flag the following for human legal, privacy, security, and procurement review:

- personal data processed outside the EEA without an identified transfer mechanism;
- less than 30 days' notice for new subprocessors;
- security-incident notification without a maximum of 24 hours;
- production-data deletion longer than 30 days after termination;
- backup retention longer than 90 days;
- unnamed subprocessors or model providers;
- ambiguous use of prompts, outputs, or derived data for model training or improvement;
- a selectable processing region that is not fixed in the signed order form.

Confidential vendor packages may be sent only to the approved Anthropic destination through Talon. OpenAI is available to other company use cases but is not approved for confidential vendor-contract review.

The generated review is advisory. It does not determine legal compliance and must not approve or reject a vendor automatically.
